Compliant is not the same as audit-ready

An examiner samples what exists, not what was intended.

Compliant

The control is described

  • A policy exists
  • A setting is turned on
  • The team believes it works
Audit-ready

The control can be shown

  • Evidence covers the full audit window
  • Any sample can be retrieved on request
  • Each record names a user or an agent
  • Recovery has been tested, not assumed

The Shared Responsibility Model

Salesforce operates the platform. Your org's configuration, retention, and evidence are yours to run.

Salesforce-operated
  • Platform infrastructure and its availability
  • Independent audit reports and certifications, published on compliance.salesforce.com
  • The logging, encryption, and backup tools you can turn on
Customer-operated
  • Which events and fields are logged
  • How long logs and history are kept
  • Who can invoke which agent, and on which data
  • Backup scope and restore testing
  • Producing the evidence an examiner samples

The questions an auditor, executive leader, or security team will ask

The same five questions appear under different names in nearly every request.

Access

Who accessed what?

Which user, integration, or agent accessed which data, and where is the log?

Change

Who changed what?

Who changed a sensitive field, when, and from what value?

Recovery

Can it be recovered?

If data is gone, how fast is it recovered, and when was that last tested?

Encryption

What is protected?

What is encrypted, and who holds the keys?

Agents

Who invoked which agent?

Which user invoked which agent, on which data, and how can you prove that?

Leave with an evidence pack, not a pitch

An evidence readiness session takes the ask you are facing, tests it the way an examiner would, and names the two or three gaps that would fail it.

  • A ranked gap list, tied to the audit, renewal, or questionnaire date
  • A completed inventory of the proof you have and the proof you assume
  • A product map for each gap, and reusable answers for questionnaires
